# MoveGap: pitch content (8 slides)

The example is fictional. Replace the demo and team status after the final production check. Do not claim interviews, sales, or verified willingness to pay.

## 1. MoveGap
**Can I afford the move on the days I must pay?**

A 60-day cash plan for renters comparing actual housing and moving offers.

## 2. The renter's cash gap
An employed renter may afford the total move yet run short before the next paycheck.

Old rent continues. A new deposit, first rent, and moving service arrive close together. The old deposit may come back later.

## 3. One move, two payment schedules
Fictional case: $4,500 opening cash, $500 desired reserve.

Original schedule: lowest balance **-$1,150**. Gap to reserve: **$1,650**.

Agreed December 4 schedule: lowest balance **$1,100**.

The total expenses are unchanged. The timing of confirmed payments changes the outcome. MoveGap does not negotiate terms.

## 4. What the product does
1. Enter cash, paydays, bills, and up to three real housing and moving options.
2. Compare compatible combinations on the same 60-day horizon.
3. Select a plan, save it in the browser, and replace estimates with actual payments.

The product shows the first problem date, lowest balance, and the payments behind it.

## 5. Live product demonstration
Use the production site, not a scripted mockup.

Change opening cash or one payment date. Compare the result. Select a plan. Record an actual payment. Save, reload, and show the updated forecast.

Screenshot space is intentionally reserved until the interface is final.

## 6. Why this workflow matters
Moving cost calculators estimate a total. Budget apps track transactions and scheduled bills.

MoveGap combines entered housing and moving offers, checks date compatibility, explains the cash gap, and tracks the chosen move.

This is a focused workflow hypothesis, not a claim of unique technology.

## 7. Commercial hypothesis
Proposed **$9.99 one-time Move Pass** for extended comparison and an updateable plan.

Users may return when a quote changes, a payment clears, or an old deposit arrives. Demand fades after the move.

The hackathon demo collects no payment. Price and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

## 8. What is proven, what comes next
Product proof: a user can enter a case, compare, save, reload, and record a payment without signing in. Verify this against the final build before presenting.

Next: observe real renters planning a move, measure completion and repeat use, then test the paid offer.
